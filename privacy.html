Privacy Policy

Fiona is built to feel like a private suite — soft, intentional, and respectful of what you share. This page explains what we collect, how AI is used, and where your preferences live.

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Information Collected

Fiona only gathers what she needs to support you in the moment. Depending on how you use the suite, that may include:

  • Optional photos you upload or capture for Glamour Suite styling and makeup analysis.
  • Mood and daily reflections you choose to log — such as Start My Day energy, That’s a Wrap check-ins, diary notes, and similar self-reflections.
  • Basic device information needed to run the experience (for example browser type, screen basics, and standard technical logs for reliability and security).

Photos and reflections are optional. You decide what to share.

2. AI Processing

When you request live style or beauty guidance, photos and relevant mood or preference context may be processed securely through Anthropic Claude APIs to generate personalized advice.

We treat that material as private suite conversation, not marketing fuel:

  • Your photos are not sold.
  • Your photos are not shared with third-party advertisers.
  • Your photos are not used to train public models.

Processing happens to return your look, palette, or reflection support — then the request path ends. We do not run an ad network against your Glamour canvas.

3. Local Storage

Many style logs, VIP preference flags, energy check-ins, ritual progress, and similar preferences are saved locally on your device (for example in your browser’s local storage). That keeps your rhythm close at hand when you return to the suite.

Clearing site data in your browser, switching devices, or using private browsing may reset those local preferences. Photos you keep in canvas features are intended to stay on-device unless you choose a live analysis request.
