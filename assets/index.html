Plumage Down Zero Effort • 5-15 min

Headboard Legs Elevation

Lie flat on back, swing heels up the headboard or wall, hands resting on belly.

"The royal shortcut to a circulation reboot. Let gravity do 100% of the heavy lifting."

Nestled Pillow Supported • 3-8 min

Pillow Mountain Chest Opener

Two pillows lengthwise along your upper spine. Head supported, arms open wide like relaxed wings.

"Opens tight rib cages constricted by pain without forcing your back into arching."

Plumage Down Side-Lying Hug • 5-10 min

Cocoon Side-Lying Hug

Curled on your favorite side, hug a firm duvet or bolster to your chest with a pillow between knees.

"Relieves lower spine tension and signals your nervous system that you are totally safe."

Fluffing Up Gentle Micro-Moves • 3 min

Bedside Jaw & Temple Sweep

Gentle fingertips gliding down from hairline, over cheekbones, releasing involuntary jaw clench.

"Pain makes us bite down in our sleep. Let that jaw hang open softly, honey."

The Yin Studio

Long, passive floor holds to restore connective tissue and melt frozen postures.

Hold Timer: 02:00
Reclined Butterfly (Supta Baddha Konasana) 2 - 5 min

Soles together, knees heavy. Place cushions under outer knees to completely remove joint pull.

"Release the hips where all your unsaid 'I'm fines' are hiding."

Sphinx Pose (Salamba Bhujangasana) 2 - 3 min

Lie face down, prop on forearms with elbows beneath shoulders. Let the lower back sag softly.

"A therapeutic decompression for your lumbar spine after hours of desk hunch."

Supported Child's Pose (Balasana) 3 - 5 min

Big toes together, knees wide. Fold forward over a thick pillow, resting one ear at a time.

"Pure surrender. No one expects anything from you here."

Bananasana (Side Meridian Stretch) 2 min / side

Lie flat on back, curve legs and arms toward the right into a gentle crescent banana shape.

"Fluffs open your intercostal ribs for fuller, deeper oxygen."
